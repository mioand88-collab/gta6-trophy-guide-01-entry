      Sources & method

      Trophy names, descriptions and grades come from the official PlayStation 5 list, as listed by PSNProfiles and Exophase (18 November 2026). Hidden flags follow the PSNProfiles listing. Counts the game tracks — 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 100 vehicles, 100 WAiNK spots, 9 gangs, 8 garages — come from that same official list. Story detail is drawn from Rockstar Games' GTA VI page and its two trailers. Every guide above is written in our own words; a source is cited beside each one, never copied.

      Missable means a trophy is tied to one specific story moment or one-time opportunity. The fix is a manual save before that moment, or mission replay from the pause menu. This guide works fully offline: it fetches nothing and loads only its own files.
